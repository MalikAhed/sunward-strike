### Appearance work still visible

- **Cloud distance/scale is explicitly unresolved.** Current n2 clouds read low and near the map. Latest in-map references require distant layered banks behind roofs, richer azure, cream/lavender underforms and broken wisps; peach/lavender mountains must separate the horizon from map geometry. Transparent distant scenery cards or a seamless sky/panorama are permitted future approaches, not implemented claims.
- Tree reference requires rounded broadleaf masses with scalloped leaf groups, golden-lime lit crowns and deep teal shadow interiors. Existing vegetation needs a fresh whole-scene comparison.
- Native n2 replaces the earlier grey sky and improves cloud shape, but cream/vegetation remain desaturated and warm-sun/cool-shade hierarchy weaker than the four references. Exact browser color-management parity remains unverified.
- Dense edge grass improves detail, but lawn centers remain comparatively flat because interior sampling is sparse. Judge distribution after final lighting, not from the close-up alone.
- RC1 reversible coral/cream materials preserve texture/normal bindings and geometry, but latest in-map tones now favor sunny-yellow siding and stronger turquoise/cream. This is a remaining appearance difference to address through reversible materials, not a reason to change measured house/lot geometry.
- Furniture R3 ProxyFixed closes misplaced lamp proxies: independent saved-source tests contain every group vertex, both lamps 64/64, with positive volumes and clear 1.1m portal samples. R2 mustard/teal/rug/art/lamp/backsplash appearance is accepted as an increment. It is **excluded from RC1**, pending a separately frozen integration and full controller/export recheck. See [furniture correction](structure-review/interiors-r3-independent.json).
